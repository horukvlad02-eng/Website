How a dental implant works

A new tooth is built from the bone up.

An implant replaces the whole tooth — root included. Each part has one job, and they go in the same order every time.

  1. 1

    The crown

    The part you see and chew with — shaped and shaded to match the teeth beside it.

  2. 2

    The abutment

    A small connector that rises through the gum and holds the crown firmly on the post.

  3. 3

    The titanium post

    Placed in the jaw where the root used to be. Over the following months, bone grows tight around it.

  4. 4

    Healthy gum tissue

    Seals around the abutment like it does around a natural tooth. Keeping it healthy is part of the plan.

  5. 5

    Your jawbone

    The foundation. We check how much bone is there before recommending an implant.

Ask about an implant consultation Every mouth is different — the exam tells us whether an implant is the right fit for you.

  1. You reach out

    Call, walk in or send a request. Tell us what is going on and which office suits you.
