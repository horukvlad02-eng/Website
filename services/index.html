Our services

Care for the mouth you actually have.

Five areas of dentistry under one roof in Northwest Houston. Your dentist explains what each option involves before any of it starts.

Family dentistry Exams, cleanings and preventive care for toddlers to grandparents — one family, one office, one plan. Cosmetic dentistry Whitening, veneers, bonding and tooth-colored fillings — planned around your face, not a template. Dental implants Replacing a missing tooth is a long-term decision. It starts with an exam, X-rays and an honest conversation about whether an implant is right for you. Restorative dentistry Fillings, crowns, bridges, root canals and gum treatment — the work of repairing and protecting what is already there. Dental emergencies Pain does not wait for a form. When something hurts or breaks, call the office — that is the fastest way in.

The process

How a visit works here

  1. You ask

    Send a request online or call the office. Tell us what is going on and which days suit you.

  2. We look

    An exam and X-rays show what is actually there. No treatment is decided from a website.

  3. You decide

    You get the options, the sequence and the costs in plain language, then you choose.

What this site will not do

  • Diagnose anything. A dentist has to see you.
  • Quote a price from a web page. Estimates come after an exam.
  • Collect medical details in a form. Keep health questions for your appointment.
