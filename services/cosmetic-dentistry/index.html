Our services

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, porcelain veneers and bonding for stains, chips, gaps and teeth that have changed with time.

  • Teeth whitening

    Both in-office and take-home whitening are offered.

  • Porcelain veneers

    For crooked, misshapen or gapped teeth — results in a few appointments.

  • Dental bonding

    Tooth-colored material to repair a chip or reshape an edge.

  • A plan, not a pitch

    Your dentist explains the options for your teeth before anything starts.

The process

How a visit works here

  1. You reach out

    Call, walk in or send a request. Tell us what is going on and which office suits you.

  2. We take a look

    An exam and digital X-rays show what is actually there. Nothing is decided from a website.

  3. You decide

    You get the options and the reasons in plain language — only necessary work is recommended.

Questions
