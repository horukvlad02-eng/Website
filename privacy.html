Privacy notice

How we handle what you send us.

What we collect. When you request an appointment we receive the details you type: your name, phone number, optional email, preferred days and times, and any note you add.

Why. Only to call or text you back about your appointment request. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

Health information. This form isn’t meant for your medical history. Please don’t include detailed health information; we’ll go over that with you privately at your visit.

Analytics. The site may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to count visits and clicks on the phone number.

Questions or deletion requests. Call us at 713-766-5804.
